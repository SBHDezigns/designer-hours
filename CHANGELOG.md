# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — first public release
- Time tracker: clock in and out, or add time by hand, for each company.
- Estimates, invoices and statements (one page showing everything a client owes).
- One-tap PDF export, including from a phone's home screen.
- Payments, partial payments and a preferred payment method per company and per invoice.
- Confirmation screens before anything is deleted or can't be undone.
- Brand settings in two small files: `public/brand.css` (fonts and colors) and `public/brand.js` (name, logo, credit).
- Optional private cloud sync behind a Cloudflare Access login, or browser-only storage.
- Built-in sample data so you can try it before you enter anything.
